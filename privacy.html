Privacy Policy

Sanctuary YouTube Publisher is a personal tool used by one person, the owner of the YouTube channel "Nobody Told Me About My Smart Home", to manage that channel.

What it accesses

Through Google OAuth and the YouTube Data and YouTube Analytics APIs, it accesses only the owner's own channel: uploading the owner's videos and reading the owner's channel and video statistics. It does not access any other person's data.

How data is used and stored

Data is used solely by the owner to publish videos and review channel performance. OAuth tokens and any retrieved data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of YouTube API data is also governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
